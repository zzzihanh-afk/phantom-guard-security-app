# PHANTOM GUARD — Official User Manual

## 1. Introduction
Phantom Guard is an enterprise-grade anti-theft and device management platform designed to protect your Android smartphone from theft, unauthorized tampering, and loss.

## 2. Getting Started
1. **Account Registration:**
   - Launch Phantom Guard on your device.
   - Register using your email address and a strong password.
   - Optional: Link your Google Account for single sign-on.
2. **Device Enrollment:**
   - On the onboarding screen, choose **"Protect This Device"**.
   - Review and grant necessary permissions:
     - **Location Permission:** For GPS tracking when requested by you.
     - **Device Administrator:** For remote locking and tamper detection.
     - **Notification & Background:** To maintain protection during standby.
3. **Activating Deep Protection (Optional):**
   - For maximum protection against uninstallation and factory reset, activate **Device Owner mode** following the ADB or QR instructions in the Setup Guide.

## 3. Remote Dashboard Management
If your phone is lost or stolen:
1. Open any computer or mobile browser.
2. Navigate to your Phantom Guard Web Dashboard.
3. Log in with your account credentials.
4. Select the missing device from the **"My Devices"** list.
5. You can immediately:
   - **Locate Device:** View exact real-time GPS position on interactive map.
   - **Remote Lock:** Lock phone with a custom contact banner.
   - **Ring Alarm:** Sound maximum volume siren even if phone is on silent.
   - **Engage Stolen Mode:** Lock down phone and monitor location continuously.
   - **Recovery Chat:** Communicate securely with whoever found your device.
   - **Wipe Device:** As a last resort, securely erase personal data.
